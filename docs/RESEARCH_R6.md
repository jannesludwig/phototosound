# Research for round 6: "The Transmission"

These are short notes on how these artists create awe with minimal means,
and what that means for this piece. Sources are listed at the end. Where
the web turned up only festival texts or interviews, I say so, and the rest
is my own knowledge of the works.

## The artists

**Ryoji Ikeda** (*datamatics*, *data.tron*, *test pattern*, *superposition*)

- **Beauty vs the sublime.** Ikeda's own definition (2011): "beauty is
  crystal: rationality, precision, simplicity, elegance, delicacy; the
  sublime is infinity: infinitesimal, immensity, indescribable, ineffable."
  The piece needs both: the precise, crystalline frame, and a moment that
  opens to infinity.
- **Reduction.** *test pattern* turns any data (text, sound, photos) into
  barcodes and binary patterns of 0s and 1s.
- **Scale comes from contrast, not from many elements.** One white field
  against black, one sine against silence, a frame-exact cut.
- **Sound and image as one signal.** A cut in the image *is* a cut in the
  sound, at the same frame.
- **The quantum framing.** *superposition* is built on quantum mechanics.
  It uses measurement, states and collapse as structure, not as decoration.

**Carsten Nicolai / Alva Noto** (*unidisplay*, *syn chron*, *Unieqav*, "Uni Mia")

- **Test signals as material.** Clicks, sines and noise bursts, treated as
  objects in space.
- **The single line or strip.** *unidisplay* is a long horizontal display:
  minimal geometry, large scale.
- **The sound is deep, dry and exact,** with a lot of air between events.
  The groove comes from micro-structure (clicks, dropouts), not from a kick
  pattern.

**Ryoichi Kurokawa** (*subassemblies*, *unfold*)

- **Entropy as the dramaturgy.** Kurokawa describes nature as an
  irreversible flow from order to chaos. Built structures are temporary
  order against that flow.
- **Material is decomposed and re-assembled into modules.** In
  *subassemblies* that material is laser scans, thermal images and footage.
  The hybrid makes "order and chaos merge into a single vision".
- **Takeaway:** one source, decomposed into several modes (spectra), then
  pushed into entropy. That is exactly acts 2 and 3.

**Robert Henke** (*Lumière I–III*)

- **Reduction.** Lasers on black: a handful of lines and points, precise
  timing, and a lot of darkness.
- **Audiences describe awe.** In Melbourne, a reviewer reports the crowd
  releasing "a collective sigh of complete amazement" when the house lights
  came up.
- **Takeaway:** awe comes from precision plus darkness plus sync, not from
  density.

**Kurt Hentschläger** (*ZEE*, *FEED*)

- **Total immersion and disorientation.** Darkness, fog and stroboscopic
  light, so that the audience completes the image in their own perception.
- **Takeaway:** the strongest moment can be almost *nothing*. A sub
  pressure in the dark before the flash is more powerful than one more
  layer.

**Semiconductor** (*Magnetic Movie*, *20 Hz*)

- **Scientific data and instruments made audible and visible.** The
  measurement *is* the aesthetic.
- **Takeaway:** keep the technical annotations (coordinates, elevation,
  frequency) and treat them as instrument readouts, not as decoration.

**Quayola** (*Strata*, *Iconographies*)

- **Classical images analysed into geometry,** in layers that drift
  between the recognisable and the abstract.
- **Takeaway:** keep the photo *almost* recognisable for a long time. The
  tension lives in that threshold.

**Daniel Rozin / Rafael Lozano-Hemmer** (interaction)

- **The viewer becomes the image.** Rozin's mirrors are built around
  participation and physicality. He admits his pieces are "very boring
  when there's not a person in front of them".
- **Takeaway:** our piece must stand on its own. Interaction is a lens,
  not the engine.
- **The critique.** Interaction that only translates the viewer's movement
  into motion, with no autonomy, is limited. Ours should change the
  *state* (observing collapses it), not only displace it.

## What this means for "The Transmission"

### Composition

| Rule | In practice |
|---|---|
| One protagonist at a time | each act has a clear main system; the others are traces |
| Black is the material | at least 60–70 % of the frame is black in every act |
| One accent colour | the photo's own colours appear only where the photo is being measured or dissolved, never as a decorative gradient |
| Thin lines and points only | no bloom, no soft glow fields, no lens flares, no rainbow |

### Pacing

- **Long holds, sudden changes.** Acts breathe for many bars, and a
  transition happens in under a second (slicing) or as a slow morph. No
  constant medium-speed motion: that is the screensaver look.
- **Silence before scale.** The bar before the strongest moment is
  near-silent, so the flash and the sub are felt.
- **The arc is irreversible: order → decomposition → entropy → collapse.**
  Never loop back to an earlier, more intact state. Round 5's photo
  popping up before the end broke this.

### Transitions

- **Morph** when the geometry is shared. The terrain flattens back onto
  the image plane while the next spectrum grows out of the same pixels.
- **Quantum slicing** when the state changes. The frame is cut into
  horizontal bands displaced against each other for a fraction of a
  second: Ikeda's cut, but spatial.
- **The camera moves only at act boundaries.** It pulls back as the image
  is decomposed and falls toward the singularity at the end.

### Awe with minimal means

- **Scale:** the camera pulling back from the photo and the singularity
  swallowing everything.
- **Precision:** frame-exact sync between clicks and their visual
  consequence.
- **The ineffable:** the end is darkness, sub pressure, one white frame,
  then debris. Leave the viewer with the remains, not with the image.

### Interaction

- **The cursor is an observer.** Around it, the current state collapses
  into another one (a lens). This is the quantum reading of Rozin's
  mirrors.
- **Force with inertia.** Elements are displaced and spring back, so the
  piece answers the body.
- **Sound under the cursor stays very quiet:** a low tone for brightness,
  fine clicks on edges. It is a whisper, never a feature.

### What to avoid

- **The screensaver look:** constant smooth particle drift, symmetric
  kaleidoscopes, colour cycling, everything always moving.
- **Pretty data art:** soft gradients, glow, pastel palettes, decorative
  particles that mean nothing.
- **The song:** regular kicks, heartbeat-like bass, 4/4 phrasing. Round 5
  went there; it is cut.
- **Kitsch sublime:** lens flares, starfields, slow-motion explosions,
  "epic" swells, bright tones.
- **Overload:** more than 3–4 visible systems at once. When in doubt,
  remove.
